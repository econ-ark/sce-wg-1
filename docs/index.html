Society for Computational Economics · Working Group 1

Language and formal semantics

Working Group 1 develops formal descriptions of dynamic economic models and their solution and estimation methods.

Formed at CEF 2026 in Venice · Partners: Econ-ARK and QuantEcon

01 · Why

Why the group was formed

Structural economists have no standard way to represent a dynamic model together with its computational solution. Moving a model between toolkits requires manual translation, and verifying that two implementations represent the same model can be difficult. Large language models can write and translate modeling code, but without an explicit account of what a model file means, economists must still check the result by hand.

Vision

We seek, for each toolkit, a solver-independent account of what its model files mean, written by the toolkit's own maintainers. These accounts would let economists compare implementations across toolkits and determine whether they represent the same model.

03 · How we work

Quarterly meetings

The full group meets once each quarter. Meetings alternate between two times to accommodate members in different time zones. Each meeting is recorded.

Project subgroups

Each project subgroup chooses its own meeting schedule.

Membership by invitation

The co-chairs invite new members.

Advisory group

A small advisory group comments on the group's plans. Advisors need not attend meetings or coauthor papers. Members and co-chairs make the decisions.

Code of conduct

A short code of conduct applies to meetings, repositories, and the mailing list.
